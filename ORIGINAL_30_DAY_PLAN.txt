30-DAY CONVERSATIONAL ENGLISH PRACTICE PLAN

How to use this plan: Spend 15-20 minutes each day. Read the phrases aloud, speak about the prompt for 2 minutes, then have a 5-10 minute conversation with a partner or voice assistant. Ask at least one follow-up question. Record yourself once a week and notice one improvement to make next time.

DAY 1 - Introductions
Theme: Names, hometowns, and first impressions.
Important phrases: "Hi, I'm ..."; "Where are you from?"; "It's nice to meet you."; "What do you do?"
Practice: Introduce yourself and ask three questions to get to know someone.

DAY 2 - Family and friends
Theme: Relationships and describing people.
Important phrases: "I have ... siblings."; "We're very close."; "What's your family like?"; "We've known each other for years."
Practice: Describe a friend or family member and explain why you get along.

DAY 3 - Daily routines
Theme: Habits, schedules, and frequency.
Important phrases: "I usually ..."; "I tend to ..."; "What time do you normally ...?"; "It depends on the day."
Practice: Compare a typical weekday with a weekend.

DAY 4 - Hobbies and free time
Theme: Interests and invitations.
Important phrases: "I'm into ..."; "How did you get into that?"; "I do it whenever I can."; "Would you like to join me?"
Practice: Explain a hobby and invite someone to try it.

DAY 5 - Food preferences
Theme: Likes, dislikes, and recommendations.
Important phrases: "I'm a big fan of ..."; "I can't eat ..."; "What would you recommend?"; "I've never tried that before."
Practice: Recommend a favorite dish and ask about someone else's preferences.

DAY 6 - Ordering at a cafe
Theme: Polite requests and clarifying an order.
Important phrases: "Could I get ...?"; "For here or to go?"; "Can I have that without ...?"; "That's all, thank you."
Practice: Role-play ordering a drink and snack, including one modification.

DAY 7 - Weekly review and small talk
Theme: Starting and keeping a light conversation going.
Important phrases: "How's your week going?"; "That sounds interesting."; "Tell me more about that."; "Speaking of ..."
Practice: Hold a five-minute chat using topics from Days 1-6; ask at least four follow-up questions.

DAY 8 - Weather and seasons
Theme: Weather, clothing, and plans.
Important phrases: "It's been really ... lately."; "What's the forecast?"; "I prefer ... weather."; "If it rains, we can ..."
Practice: Discuss the weather today and plan an activity for another season.

DAY 9 - Neighborhood and city
Theme: Places, atmosphere, and local tips.
Important phrases: "I live near ..."; "It's within walking distance."; "There's a great ... nearby."; "What's your neighborhood like?"
Practice: Give a newcomer three suggestions for your area.

DAY 10 - Directions and transportation
Theme: Getting around and checking understanding.
Important phrases: "How do I get to ...?"; "Go straight and turn left."; "Is it far from here?"; "So I take the next right?"
Practice: Explain how to get from your home to a familiar place.

DAY 11 - Shopping
Theme: Sizes, prices, and decisions.
Important phrases: "Do you have this in another size?"; "How much is it?"; "Can I try it on?"; "I'll think about it."
Practice: Role-play buying an item and comparing two options.

DAY 12 - Making plans
Theme: Availability and agreeing on details.
Important phrases: "Are you free on ...?"; "How about ...?"; "That works for me."; "Let's meet at ..."
Practice: Arrange a weekend outing, including time and place.

DAY 13 - Work or studies
Theme: Responsibilities, goals, and challenges.
Important phrases: "I'm working on ..."; "My main responsibility is ..."; "What are you studying?"; "The hardest part is ..."
Practice: Explain a current project in plain English and invite a question.

DAY 14 - Weekly review and storytelling
Theme: Sequencing a recent experience.
Important phrases: "At first ..."; "Then ..."; "After that ..."; "In the end ..."
Practice: Tell a two-minute story about something that happened this week; answer two follow-up questions.

DAY 15 - Travel experiences
Theme: Destinations, memories, and preferences.
Important phrases: "I've been to ..."; "I'd love to visit ..."; "The best part was ..."; "How was your trip?"
Practice: Describe a memorable trip or a place you hope to visit.

DAY 16 - Hotels and accommodation
Theme: Reservations, needs, and requests.
Important phrases: "I have a reservation under ..."; "What time is check-in?"; "Is breakfast included?"; "Could I get an extra ...?"
Practice: Role-play checking in and asking about two amenities.

DAY 17 - Health and well-being
Theme: Everyday symptoms and self-care.
Important phrases: "I'm feeling a bit ..."; "I've had a headache since ..."; "I need to rest."; "How are you feeling now?"
Practice: Explain a minor illness and respond kindly to someone who feels unwell.

DAY 18 - Exercise and fitness
Theme: Activities, goals, and routines.
Important phrases: "I try to stay active by ..."; "How often do you ...?"; "I'm trying to improve my ..."; "Let's take it easy."
Practice: Compare two activities and suggest a realistic exercise plan.

DAY 19 - Movies, books, and music
Theme: Describing tastes without spoilers.
Important phrases: "It's about ..."; "I really enjoyed ..."; "I'm not usually into ..."; "What did you think of it?"
Practice: Recommend something you enjoyed and explain who might like it.

DAY 20 - Technology and apps
Theme: Everyday tools and troubleshooting.
Important phrases: "I use it to ..."; "It's easy to set up."; "Have you tried restarting it?"; "I'm having trouble with ..."
Practice: Explain how to use a favorite app and ask for help with a simple problem.

DAY 21 - Weekly review and opinions
Theme: Giving reasons and acknowledging another view.
Important phrases: "In my opinion ..."; "I see your point."; "I prefer ... because ..."; "What makes you say that?"
Practice: Discuss two entertainment or technology choices; politely disagree once.

DAY 22 - Celebrations and traditions
Theme: Special occasions and cultural customs.
Important phrases: "We usually celebrate by ..."; "It's a tradition to ..."; "What does it mean to you?"; "That sounds like fun."
Practice: Describe a celebration and ask about one from another culture.

DAY 23 - Past experiences
Theme: Milestones, lessons, and changes.
Important phrases: "When I was younger ..."; "I used to ..."; "I learned that ..."; "Since then, I've ..."
Practice: Tell a story about learning a new skill.

DAY 24 - Future goals
Theme: Plans, hopes, and next steps.
Important phrases: "I'm planning to ..."; "I hope to ..."; "My next step is ..."; "What are you looking forward to?"
Practice: Share one short-term and one long-term goal.

DAY 25 - Solving a problem
Theme: Explaining issues and proposing solutions.
Important phrases: "The issue is ..."; "Could we try ...?"; "That might work."; "What else can we do?"
Practice: Role-play a missed appointment or delayed delivery and agree on a solution.

DAY 26 - Complaints and customer service
Theme: Staying polite and stating what you need.
Important phrases: "I'm afraid there's a problem with ..."; "I ordered ..., but received ..."; "Could you help me resolve this?"; "I appreciate your help."
Practice: Explain a wrong order and request a reasonable fix.

DAY 27 - Giving advice
Theme: Suggestions, empathy, and choices.
Important phrases: "You could ..."; "Have you considered ...?"; "If I were you, I'd ..."; "It's up to you."
Practice: Advise a friend who wants to practice English more regularly.

DAY 28 - Weekly review and longer conversation
Theme: Switching topics smoothly and repairing misunderstandings.
Important phrases: "That reminds me ..."; "By the way ..."; "What do you mean by ...?"; "Let me put it another way."
Practice: Have a ten-minute conversation covering three earlier topics; clarify one point and ask follow-up questions.

DAY 29 - Current events and community
Theme: Discussing a local event without assuming agreement.
Important phrases: "I heard about ..."; "From what I understand ..."; "How do you feel about it?"; "I haven't looked into it enough yet."
Practice: Discuss a recent community event or a noncontroversial news story; separate facts from opinions.

DAY 30 - Final conversation and reflection
Theme: Combining stories, opinions, questions, and plans.
Important phrases: "One thing I've learned is ..."; "I feel more confident about ..."; "I'd like to keep improving ..."; "What should we talk about next?"
Practice: Hold a 15-minute conversation spanning at least four topics. Record it, note three phrases you used well, and choose two skills to keep practicing.

After Day 30: Revisit the most difficult five topics, repeat the speaking prompts with new details, and keep a simple log of new phrases you use in real conversations.
